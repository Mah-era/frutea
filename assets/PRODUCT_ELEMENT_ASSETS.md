# Product and Element Assets

Generated from the current `index.html` Three.js product builders.

- Current PNG cutouts: `assets/products/v2/png/`
- Current GLB meshes: `assets/products/v2/glb/`
- Local-only archived product assets: `assets/products/v1/`

The PNG files are transparent, uncropped product/element renders. The GLB files are glTF Binary exports from the actual Three.js objects used by the page, including geometry and embedded canvas texture maps where available.

`v2` contains the approved current range: five single sachets at BDT 20, the BDT 200 Mix Pack, the planned Year 2 Low Sugar range, drinks, fruit, garnish, ice, and straw elements. `v1` preserves the former BDT 35 sachets, former Mix Pack treatment, and removed bulk-pouch assets for reference only.
