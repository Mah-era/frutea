# FruTea Motion And 3D Guideline

## Motion Personality

FruTea motion should feel light, refreshing, and product-led. Movement should help users understand flavour, serving mode, and pack variety.

## Always-On 3D Brand Element

Use an always-on FruTea scene element in the website interface:

- Keep it small enough that it does not block product copy.
- Show the current flavour pack, glass, and matching fruit cue.
- Change angle with scroll.
- Use a gentle zoom or scale change between sections.
- Switch from pack focus to serve focus in the hot/cold section.
- Keep movement smooth and continuous.

Recommended behaviour:

| Page Area | Always-On Element |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Hero flavour pack with glass |
| Flavours | Active flavour sachet plus fruit |
| Hot/Cold | Hot cup changes to cold tall glass |
| Low Sugar | Lemon or Honey-Lemon green tea product |
| Collection/CTA | Mix Pack or flavour fan |

## Scroll Transitions

Use scroll to show product variety:

- Flavour packs slide or rotate into focus one at a time.
- Drink colour changes with the active flavour.
- Fruit elements enter from the side or lower foreground.
- Mix Pack should reveal all five sachets.
- Low Sugar Lemon and Honey-Lemon should be separate scroll states.

## Glass Animation

Iced glass:

- Add floating ice at the drink surface.
- Bob ice very subtly.
- Rotate ice slowly so it catches light.
- Adjust liquid surface around the ice.
- Keep drink colour readable against the background.
- Use flavour-matched garnish.

Hot cup:

- Use small cup form.
- Add warm amber tea.
- Add steam or soft heat shimmer.
- No ice.
- No splash.

## Pack Animation

Packs can:

- Rotate slightly on scroll.
- Scale up when active.
- Move between foreground and background.
- Keep front label readable during hero moments.

Packs should not:

- Spin so far that labels become unreadable.
- Collide with the glass.
- Have dark side borders on large front-facing scenes.
- Use motion that feels like a generic tech product rather than a drink brand.

## Scene Elements

Use scene elements that are tailored to FruTea:

- Lemon slices and whole lemons.
- Mango pieces or whole mango.
- Lychee fruit.
- Orange slices and whole orange.
- Strawberry fruit.
- Mint leaves.
- Striped straw.
- Ice cubes.
- FruTea mascot where appropriate.

Avoid unrelated decorative blobs, generic splashes, or random shapes.

## Timing

| Motion Type | Recommended Feel |
| --- | --- |
| Idle floating | Slow, 4s to 7s loops |
| Pack transition | Smooth, 0.7s to 1.2s |
| Scroll scrub | Responsive but eased |
| Ice bob | Tiny, continuous, non-distracting |
| Glass colour shift | Smooth blend between flavours |

## Verification Checklist

Before final delivery:

- Product label is readable.
- Glass looks clear and refreshing.
- Ice is visible and not hidden inside the liquid.
- Drink colour contrasts with the background.
- Mix Pack includes all five actual sachet designs.
- Hot/cold segment clearly changes hot small cup to cold tall glass.
- No water splash appears in current scenes.
- Always-on 3D element is visible but not blocking the page.
