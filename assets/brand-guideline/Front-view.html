FruTea Brand Guideline

This folder contains the working brand guideline kit for FruTea Instant.

FruTea Instant is an instant fruit-flavoured tea powder brand for Bangladesh. The brand should feel fresh, energetic, colourful, youth-friendly, affordable, and easy to understand at a glance.

Files

  • brand-system.md - brand positioning, voice, colour, typography, logo, and layout rules.
  • packaging-guideline.md - rules for sachets, the Mix Pack, planned Low Sugar packs, and serving glass visuals.
  • motion-3d-guideline.md - website animation, scroll, glass, ice, product render, and always-on 3D rules.
  • asset-index.md - where to find usable logos, mascots, product renders, videos, and 3D files.
  • design-tokens.json - reusable brand colours, typography, flavour names, product data, and motion values.
  • brand-tokens.css - CSS custom properties for frontend implementation.

Core Direction

Keep FruTea product-led. The pack, glass, fruit elements, ice, straw, and mascot should do the visual work. Backgrounds can be bright, but they should not fight the drink colour or hide the product.

Use the current product data in ../../product.md as the source of truth.

brand-system.md

FruTea Instant Brand System

Brand Summary

FruTea Instant is an affordable instant fruit-flavoured tea powder. It is made to be mixed with hot or cold water and enjoyed immediately.

The brand bridges regular tea-break culture with colourful ready-to-mix fruit refreshment. It should feel modern and playful, but still commercially usable for stores, reports, presentations, websites, and packaging mockups.

Positioning

FruTea Instant is for students, young professionals, families, offices, canteens, and online grocery buyers in Bangladesh who want a colourful tea break without brewing or waiting.

Brand Promise

Quick fruit tea that can be served hot or iced.

Personality

  • Fresh
  • Fruity
  • Energetic
  • Friendly
  • Youthful
  • Affordable
  • Social
  • Colourful but not childish

Voice

Use short, clear, bright phrasing. FruTea can sound playful, but product clarity comes first.

Good Message Examples

  • Fruit tea, turned all the way up.
  • Tea break, but in full colour.
  • Tear. Pour. Stir.
  • Serve it warm or over ice.
  • Five flavours, zero dull breaks.
  • Which FruTea are you today?

Avoid

  • Overly premium or luxury wording.
  • Long scientific claims.
  • Visual or text clutter.
  • Generic soft-drink language that hides the tea identity.

Type Rules

  • Use bold, rounded, high-confidence headings.
  • Keep package labels readable from a distance.
  • Use uppercase for pack labels and flavour names when it improves shelf impact.
  • Keep letter spacing at 0 for large display text.
  • Avoid thin type on bright fruit colours.

Logo Rules

Preferred logo files live in ../Logo/versions/.

Use:

  • primary-logo.png for normal light backgrounds.
  • horizontal-logo.png for website nav and narrow placements.
  • white-reverse-logo.png for dark wine or red backgrounds.
  • logo-mark-symbol.png for small badges, seals, icons, and 3D pack marks.
  • favicon.png or app-icon.png for browser and app surfaces.

Clear Space

Leave at least the height of the FruTea leaf/mark around the logo. Do not place fruit, ice, straw, or text directly against the logo.

Do Not

  • Add dark side borders to large pack fronts.
  • Stretch or rotate the logo on flat package faces.
  • Put the logo on a low-contrast flavour colour without a cream or white backing.
  • Hide the brand in tiny nav text only. The pack or logo must be a first-screen signal.

Layout Direction

FruTea layouts should be product-first:

  • Pack on one side, serving glass on the other.
  • Fruit and ice support the flavour story.
  • Backgrounds stay clean enough for drink colour to read clearly.
  • Use fruit shapes, sachets, and glassware as scene elements instead of generic decoration.
  • Avoid water splash unless specifically approved for a campaign scene.

Photography And Render Mood

The drink should look refreshing and tempting:

  • Clear glass edges.
  • Visible ice with light highlights.
  • Drink colours that contrast against the background.
  • Fruit garnish matched to the flavour.
  • Straw colour matched or complementary.
  • Condensation can be subtle, but it should not become noisy.

FruTea Packaging Guideline

Source Product Range

Use ../../product.md as the source of truth for product names, quantity, servings, and proposed prices.

Packaging Architecture

Pack TypeProductsQuantityMain Role
Single sachetLemon, Mango, Lychee, Orange, Strawberry15gYear 1, one cup, ৳20
Mix Pack boxAssorted flavours10 sachets x 15gYear 1 discovery pack, ৳200
Low Sugar sachetLemon, Honey-Lemon12gYear 2, one cup, ৳35

Shared Pack Structure

Every pack should include:

  • FruTea logo or logo mark in a cream safe area.
  • Product type: Instant Fruit Tea or Low Sugar Green Tea.
  • Clear flavour name.
  • Pack quantity.
  • Serving count.
  • Serve cue: hot or cold.
  • Simple benefit seals such as 1 cup, 30s stir, 10 sachets, or 5 flavours.
  • Fruit or ingredient illustration matched to flavour.

Pack Design Rules

  • Keep the same flavour colours already used on the current packs.
  • Remove dark side borders from large pack fronts.
  • Use cream top bands inspired by the reference pack system.
  • Use subtle crimp lines at top and bottom for sachets.
  • Keep the flavour name large and readable.
  • Use fruit elements as designed illustrations or 3D-like renders, not random stickers.
  • Keep the pack front clean enough to read from a distance.

Single Sachets

Single sachets should feel slim, bright, and collectible.

Recommended hierarchy:

  1. Cream crimp header.
  2. FruTea logo.
  3. Instant Fruit Tea.
  4. Large flavour name.
  5. Fruit illustration near the lower third.
  6. Quantity line: 15g · one cup.

Mix Pack Box

The Mix Pack must show all five sachet designs inside the box front.

Required visual details:

  • Coral or warm FruTea family colour base.
  • Cream top panel.
  • FruTea logo mark near the top.
  • Large label: Instant Fruit Tea Mix Pack.
  • Benefit seals: 10 sachets, 5 flavours, ৳200.
  • A visible row or fan of all five sachets: Lemon, Mango, Lychee, Orange, Strawberry.
  • Bottom banner: 5 flavours · 10 cups · ৳200.

The Mix Pack should feel like a variety discovery box.

Low Sugar Green Tea Year 2 Packs

Create two separate products:

  • FruTea Low Sugar Green Tea Lemon
  • FruTea Low Sugar Green Tea Honey-Lemon

Shared rules:

  • Green tea base colour with clean lemon or honey accent.
  • Cream top panel.
  • Distinct flavour callout.
  • Lemon uses brighter citrus yellow.
  • Honey-Lemon uses warmer honey gold.
  • Include a glass or serving cue tailored to the flavour.
  • State 12g, ৳35, and Year 2 launch clearly.

Serving Glass Guideline

Iced Tall Glass

The iced glass should look refreshing and tempting:

  • Tall clear glass.
  • Drink colour matched to flavour.
  • Visible floating ice at the surface.
  • Additional submerged ice inside the drink.
  • Liquid level adjusted around ice so cubes look partly inside the drink.
  • Garnish matched to flavour.
  • Optional striped straw.

Hot Cup

The hot serve should use:

  • A smaller cup or handled glass.
  • Amber or warm tea colour.
  • Steam or heat shimmer.
  • No ice.
  • No water splash.

Background Contrast

The drink colour must not collide with the background colour. If the drink is yellow, avoid a fully yellow background behind the glass. If the drink is red, use cream, sky, mint, or warm orange offset panels to keep the glass readable.

Do Not

  • Do not add dark vertical borders to big pack fronts.
  • Do not use water splash in the current product scenes.
  • Do not let sachet images in the Mix Pack become generic colour blocks.
  • Do not use one design for Lemon and Honey-Lemon Low Sugar.
  • Do not hide the glass behind pack elements.
motion-3d-guideline.md

FruTea Motion And 3D Guideline

Motion Personality

FruTea motion should feel light, refreshing, and product-led. Movement should help users understand flavour, serving mode, and pack variety.

Always-On 3D Brand Element

Use an always-on FruTea scene element in the website interface:

  • Keep it small enough that it does not block product copy.
  • Show the current flavour pack, glass, and matching fruit cue.
  • Change angle with scroll.
  • Use a gentle zoom or scale change between sections.
  • Switch from pack focus to serve focus in the hot/cold section.
  • Keep movement smooth and continuous.

Recommended behaviour:

Page AreaAlways-On Element
HeroHero flavour pack with glass
FlavoursActive flavour sachet plus fruit
Hot/ColdHot cup changes to cold tall glass
Low SugarLemon or Honey-Lemon green tea product
Collection/CTAMix Pack or flavour fan

Scroll Transitions

Use scroll to show product variety:

  • Flavour packs slide or rotate into focus one at a time.
  • Drink colour changes with the active flavour.
  • Fruit elements enter from the side or lower foreground.
  • Mix Pack should reveal all five sachets.
  • Low Sugar Lemon and Honey-Lemon should be separate scroll states.

Glass Animation

Iced glass:

  • Add floating ice at the drink surface.
  • Bob ice very subtly.
  • Rotate ice slowly so it catches light.
  • Adjust liquid surface around the ice.
  • Keep drink colour readable against the background.
  • Use flavour-matched garnish.

Hot cup:

  • Use small cup form.
  • Add warm amber tea.
  • Add steam or soft heat shimmer.
  • No ice.
  • No splash.

Pack Animation

Packs can:

  • Rotate slightly on scroll.
  • Scale up when active.
  • Move between foreground and background.
  • Keep front label readable during hero moments.

Packs should not:

  • Spin so far that labels become unreadable.
  • Collide with the glass.
  • Have dark side borders on large front-facing scenes.
  • Use motion that feels like a generic tech product rather than a drink brand.

Scene Elements

Use scene elements that are tailored to FruTea:

  • Lemon slices and whole lemons.
  • Mango pieces or whole mango.
  • Lychee fruit.
  • Orange slices and whole orange.
  • Strawberry fruit.
  • Mint leaves.
  • Striped straw.
  • Ice cubes.
  • FruTea mascot where appropriate.

Avoid unrelated decorative blobs, generic splashes, or random shapes.

Timing

Motion TypeRecommended Feel
Idle floatingSlow, 4s to 7s loops
Pack transitionSmooth, 0.7s to 1.2s
Scroll scrubResponsive but eased
Ice bobTiny, continuous, non-distracting
Glass colour shiftSmooth blend between flavours

Verification Checklist

Before final delivery:

  • Product label is readable.
  • Glass looks clear and refreshing.
  • Ice is visible and not hidden inside the liquid.
  • Drink colour contrasts with the background.
  • Mix Pack includes all five actual sachet designs.
  • Hot/cold segment clearly changes hot small cup to cold tall glass.
  • No water splash appears in current scenes.
  • Always-on 3D element is visible but not blocking the page.
