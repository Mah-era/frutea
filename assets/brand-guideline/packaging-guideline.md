# FruTea Packaging Guideline

## Source Product Range

Use `../../product.md` as the source of truth for product names, quantity, servings, and proposed prices.

## Packaging Architecture

| Pack Type | Products | Quantity | Main Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single sachet | Lemon, Mango, Lychee, Orange, Strawberry | 15g | Trial, one cup, low price |
| Mix Pack box | Assorted flavours | 10 sachets x 15g | Discovery, sharing, variety |
| Low Sugar sachet | Lemon, Honey-Lemon | 12g | Year 2 green tea launch, one cup |

## Shared Pack Structure

Every pack should include:

- FruTea logo or logo mark in a cream safe area.
- Product type: `Instant Fruit Tea` or `Low Sugar Green Tea`.
- Clear flavour name.
- Pack quantity.
- Serving count.
- Serve cue: hot or cold.
- Simple benefit seals such as `1 cup`, `30s stir`, `10 sachets`, or `5 flavours`.
- Fruit or ingredient illustration matched to flavour.

## Pack Design Rules

- Keep the same flavour colours already used on the current packs.
- Remove dark side borders from large pack fronts.
- Use cream top bands inspired by the reference pack system.
- Use subtle crimp lines at top and bottom for sachets.
- Keep the flavour name large and readable.
- Use fruit elements as designed illustrations or 3D-like renders, not random stickers.
- Keep the pack front clean enough to read from a distance.

## Single Sachets

Single sachets should feel slim, bright, and collectible.

Recommended hierarchy:

1. Cream crimp header.
2. FruTea logo.
3. `Instant Fruit Tea`.
4. Large flavour name.
5. Fruit illustration near the lower third.
6. Quantity line: `15g · one cup`.

## Mix Pack Box

The Mix Pack must show all five sachet designs inside the box front.

Required visual details:

- Coral or warm FruTea family colour base.
- Cream top panel.
- FruTea logo mark near the top.
- Large label: `Instant Fruit Tea Mix Pack`.
- Benefit seals: `10 sachets`, `5 flavours`, `৳150`.
- A visible row or fan of all five sachets: Lemon, Mango, Lychee, Orange, Strawberry.
- Bottom banner: `5 flavours · 10 cups · ৳150`.

The Mix Pack should feel like a variety discovery box, not a single-flavour pack.

## Low Sugar Green Tea Year 2 Packs

Create two separate products:

- FruTea Low Sugar Green Tea Lemon
- FruTea Low Sugar Green Tea Honey-Lemon

Shared rules:

- Green tea base colour with clean lemon or honey accent.
- Cream top panel.
- Distinct flavour callout.
- Lemon uses brighter citrus yellow.
- Honey-Lemon uses warmer honey gold.
- Include a glass or serving cue tailored to the flavour.
- State `12g`, `৳35`, and `Year 2 launch` clearly.
- Present the range behind a coming-soon reveal until Year 1 is complete.

## Serving Glass Guideline

### Iced Tall Glass

The iced glass should look refreshing and tempting:

- Tall clear glass.
- Drink colour matched to flavour.
- Visible floating ice at the surface.
- Additional submerged ice inside the drink.
- Liquid level adjusted around ice so cubes look partly inside the drink.
- Garnish matched to flavour.
- Optional striped straw.

### Hot Cup

The hot serve should use:

- A smaller cup or handled glass.
- Amber or warm tea colour.
- Steam or heat shimmer.
- No ice.
- No water splash.

### Background Contrast

The drink colour must not collide with the background colour. If the drink is yellow, avoid a fully yellow background behind the glass. If the drink is red, use cream, sky, mint, or warm orange offset panels to keep the glass readable.

## Do Not

- Do not add dark vertical borders to big pack fronts.
- Do not use water splash in the current product scenes.
- Do not let sachet images in the Mix Pack become generic colour blocks.
- Do not use one design for Lemon and Honey-Lemon Low Sugar.
- Do not hide the glass behind pack elements.
