# FruTea Instant Brand System

## Brand Summary

FruTea Instant is an affordable instant fruit-flavoured tea powder. It is made to be mixed with hot or cold water and enjoyed immediately.

The brand bridges regular tea-break culture with colourful ready-to-mix fruit refreshment. It should feel modern and playful, but still commercially usable for stores, reports, presentations, websites, and packaging mockups.

## Positioning

FruTea Instant is for students, young professionals, families, offices, canteens, and online grocery buyers in Bangladesh who want a colourful tea break without brewing or waiting.

## Brand Promise

Quick fruit tea that can be served hot or iced.

## Personality

- Fresh
- Fruity
- Energetic
- Friendly
- Youthful
- Affordable
- Social
- Colourful but not childish

## Voice

Use short, clear, bright phrasing. FruTea can sound playful, but product clarity comes first.

### Good Message Examples

- Fruit tea, turned all the way up.
- Tea break, but in full colour.
- Tear. Pour. Stir.
- Serve it warm or over ice.
- Five flavours, zero dull breaks.
- Which FruTea are you today?

### Avoid

- Overly premium or luxury wording.
- Long scientific claims.
- Visual or text clutter.
- Generic soft-drink language that hides the tea identity.

## Typography

| Font | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Fredoka | Big headings, friendly brand moments |
| Poppins | Product names, labels, buttons, prices, pack information |
| Nunito Sans | Body copy and supporting text |
| Swipe or expressive flavour style | Large flavour words and playful emphasis |

### Type Rules

- Use bold, rounded, high-confidence headings.
- Keep package labels readable from a distance.
- Use uppercase for pack labels and flavour names when it improves shelf impact.
- Keep letter spacing at `0` for large display text.
- Avoid thin type on bright fruit colours.

## Logo Rules

Preferred logo files live in `../Logo/versions/`.

Use:

- `primary-logo.png` for normal light backgrounds.
- `horizontal-logo.png` for website nav and narrow placements.
- `white-reverse-logo.png` for dark wine or red backgrounds.
- `logo-mark-symbol.png` for small badges, seals, icons, and 3D pack marks.
- `favicon.png` or `app-icon.png` for browser and app surfaces.

### Clear Space

Leave at least the height of the FruTea leaf/mark around the logo. Do not place fruit, ice, straw, or text directly against the logo.

### Do Not

- Add dark side borders to large pack fronts.
- Stretch or rotate the logo on flat package faces.
- Put the logo on a low-contrast flavour colour without a cream or white backing.
- Hide the brand in tiny nav text only. The pack or logo must be a first-screen signal.

## Layout Direction

FruTea layouts should be product-first:

- Pack on one side, serving glass on the other.
- Fruit and ice support the flavour story.
- Backgrounds stay clean enough for drink colour to read clearly.
- Use fruit shapes, sachets, and glassware as scene elements instead of generic decoration.
- Avoid water splash unless specifically approved for a campaign scene.

## Photography And Render Mood

The drink should look refreshing and tempting:

- Clear glass edges.
- Visible ice with light highlights.
- Drink colours that contrast against the background.
- Fruit garnish matched to the flavour.
- Straw colour matched or complementary.
- Condensation can be subtle, but it should not become noisy.
