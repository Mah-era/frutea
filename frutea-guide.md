# FruTea Instant Brand & Product Guide

## 1. Brand Overview

**Company / Brand Name:** FruTea Instant
**Product Category:** Instant fruit-flavoured tea powder
**Core Offer:** A quick tear-and-stir fruit tea powder that can be served hot or iced.
**Primary Market:** Bangladesh
**Target Customers:** Students, young professionals, families, offices, canteens, and online grocery buyers.

FruTea Instant is a modern, colourful beverage brand built around fast preparation, fruit-led refreshment, and accessible pricing. The brand should feel energetic, youthful, friendly, and practical. It is not positioned as a premium luxury tea brand; it is positioned as an affordable everyday drink that makes ordinary tea breaks feel more exciting.

## 2. Brand Positioning

FruTea Instant sits between traditional tea culture and modern ready-to-mix beverages. The product gives customers a familiar tea-break ritual but updates it with bright fruit flavours, instant preparation, and hot-or-iced flexibility.

**Positioning Statement:**
FruTea Instant is an affordable instant fruit tea powder for people in Bangladesh who want a colourful, refreshing tea break without brewing, waiting, or complicated preparation.

**Brand Promise:**
Fruit tea that is quick, colourful, and easy to enjoy anywhere.

**Key Differentiators:**

- Instant preparation: tear, pour, stir, drink.
- Works hot or iced.
- Five core fruit flavours.
- Entry price starts at ৳20.
- Available at launch in single sachets and the Mix Pack; Low Sugar Green Tea follows in Year 2.
- Youth-friendly design with bold fruit colours and energetic visuals.

## 3. Brand Personality

FruTea Instant should feel:

- Fresh
- Fruity
- Energetic
- Cheerful
- Modern
- Youth-friendly
- Affordable
- Social
- Colourful but not childish
- Modern beverage-style but accessible

The brand voice should be lively and direct. It can use playful phrasing, but it should still remain clear enough for product, report, and business presentation use.

## 5. Product Summary

FruTea Instant is an instant fruit-flavoured tea powder. Customers mix the powder with hot or cold water, stir, and drink instantly.

**Preparation Method:**

1. Tear the sachet or open the pack.
2. Pour into hot or cold water.
3. Stir and serve immediately.

**Serving Modes:**

- Hot fruit tea for comfort and everyday tea breaks.
- Iced fruit tea for warm weather and refreshment.

## 8. Packaging System

FruTea packaging should be bold, colourful, and easy to recognize quickly. Each flavour should have its own strong colour while keeping a shared brand structure.

**Core Pack Types:**

- 15g single sachet
- 10-sachet Mix Pack box
- 12g Low Sugar Green Tea sachet, planned for Year 2

**Packaging Content Priorities:**

- FruTea logo
- Instant label
- Flavour name
- Product type: fruit tea / green tea
- Serve hot or cold
- Quantity
- Serving count
- Proposed price
- Fruit illustration or fruit render
- Simple benefit seals such as `15g sachet`, `1 cup`, `30s stir`

## 9. Visual Identity

The visual identity should be fresh, bright, and fruit-led. It should feel like a modern youth beverage brand with a summer mood and clean product focus.

**Visual Style Rules:**

- Use fresh fruit colours.
- Keep backgrounds mostly clean.
- Use fruit splash visuals, tea glasses, sachet mockups, ice cubes, and water drops.
- Use rounded shapes and soft curves to make the brand feel friendly.
- Use bold product photos or 3D product renders in hero sections.
- Keep typography clean, modern, and easy to read.
- Use small icons for instant, fruity, refreshing, hot/cold, and affordable.

## 17. Design Do's

- Make the product visible early.
- Use real product mockups, 3D renders, or product cutouts.
- Keep fruit colours vibrant and distinct.
- Keep content simple and scannable.
- Use bold flavour names.
- Use clear pack and price information.
- Show hot and iced usage.
- Keep the brand youthful but still commercially usable.

## 18. Design Don'ts

- Do not make the brand look too formal or traditional.
- Do not use dull, muted, single-colour layouts.
- Do not make the product feel expensive or inaccessible.
- Do not hide price and pack information.
- Do not make visuals childish despite the playful colour system.
- Do not rely only on text; the product needs strong visual presence.
- Do not use dark, heavy backgrounds that make the drink feel less refreshing.

## 19. Competitive Advantage

FruTea Instant’s advantage is its combination of speed, fruit flavour, price accessibility, and flexible serving style. It can compete as a fun alternative to regular tea, powdered drinks, soft drinks, and iced beverages.

**Strongest Selling Points:**

- Instant preparation
- Hot and iced use
- Five flavour range
- Entry price of ৳20
- Mix Pack trial format
- Distinctive colourful design system

## 20. Final Brand Direction

FruTea Instant should be presented as a bright, modern, affordable beverage brand for Bangladesh. The final design direction should combine strong product visuals, fruit-colour energy, simple preparation messaging, and clear pricing. The brand should always feel refreshing, quick, social, and easy to choose.

**One-line Summary:**
FruTea Instant turns a normal tea break into a colourful hot-or-iced fruit tea moment.
